# Pendiva impact statement

## The problem hypothesis

Small service businesses may coordinate customer requests, quote questions, schedule details, and follow-ups across chat threads. When operational facts are buried in messages, owners may need to search manually or rely on memory. This is a problem hypothesis to validate with a wider set of businesses, not a quantified finding about all service companies.

## Who Pendiva is designed for

Owner-operators and small teams in service businesses such as cleaning, maintenance, repair, and other appointment- or quote-based work. The current demonstration uses a fictional cleaning company and is not evidence of fit across these sectors.

## How Pendiva may help

Pendiva organizes messages delivered to its webhook into customer workflows, uses Muse Spark to suggest follow-ups with source messages attached, and makes the owner responsible for reviewing each suggestion. This may reduce the effort to find context and make missing details easier to notice. No time saving, conversion increase, revenue recovery, or customer satisfaction outcome has been measured.

## Evidence available

- A sandbox text message was received and stored once in a dated test; that historical event does not establish current connectivity or complete message-history coverage.
- A small synthetic Muse evaluation showed evidence-linked suggestions and also revealed amount ambiguity and weak next-step phrasing.
- The repository includes synthetic conversations and finance fixtures to make the demo repeatable.
- The project notes two interviews and one verbal interest signal reported by the owner, but interview details and a broader sample are not documented in a way that supports a market conclusion.

## Responsible impact measurement

For a future opt-in pilot, measure the time required to find the customer request, percentage of suggestions accepted/edited/dismissed, unsupported claim rate, follow-ups completed, and owner-rated usefulness. Report the sample size, baseline, duration, and failures. Keep attribution cautious: a follow-up occurring after an AI suggestion does not by itself prove that the suggestion caused a sale.

## Validation still needed

Conduct documented discovery with service-business owners, confirm customer-data permissions and retention expectations, run an independently reviewed bilingual evaluation, validate WhatsApp history and outbound-message coverage with an eligible test setup, and measure usability on real workflows only with informed consent and appropriate safeguards.
